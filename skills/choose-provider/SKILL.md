# Choose provider

Use find_providers for the ticket trade and property/postcode. Exclude suspended
and do_not_use. Gas work requires current Gas Safe; electrical work requires
current NICEIC or NAPIT. Check insurance/accreditation expiry and actual
availability directly: emergency_callout and response estimates are not promises.
For emergencies prioritise attendance time; routine work balances sourced price,
rating and first-time fix rate. Recheck prices older than six months. Seek 2-3
quotes above the managing company's cost threshold; absent a threshold seek
quotes rather than inventing policy. record_quote captures scope/exclusions,
VAT and dates; accept_quote assigns the provider and records an expected
expense, never a payment. Record financial decisions with skill=choose-provider.
