# Provider research

When coverage is missing or prices are over six months old, browse provider
websites with the existing browser service. Check service area, trade,
booking instructions, emergency terms, accreditation and insurance. Record
providers using phone/email/company-number resolution keys. Record each
offering separately with price_source=website, last_confirmed_at, explicit VAT,
scope/exclusions and source evidence. Do not infer accreditation or availability
from an advert alone; verify required credentials and attendance with the firm.
Keep bank account details out of the graph and never initiate payments.
